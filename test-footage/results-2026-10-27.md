## 1. busy-warehouse

**r02, missed over the whole clip.** The condition is true only on s24 (the operator gets out of the cab). The cause is the convention "`in_zone` by footpoint". For the seated operator the bottom edge of the box runs along the cab (y 337–438 px), not along the floor. This point lies above the `past_gate` border below it (y 424–500 px), that is, "in the gate opening". SAM found no hard hat and no vest on any frame (as expected), so only the zone matters. A what-if check: if the operator's footpoint (the person who counts as the operator by the rule below) is moved to the bottom edge of the forklift box, r02 gives exactly s2–s24. This is not in the engine. `r02` has no `forklift` object, so the engine has nothing to rely on (options in "Summary").

**r05.** No events, as expected (60 s window). But the candidate is wrong. It was expected on s0–s5 (a pallet on the forks at the gate) and came out as s5 and s9–s24. On a clip longer than 60 s this would be a false alert. SAM did not find the wrapped load on the forks as a `pallet` or `box` in the zone.

## Engine fixes (27.10)

1. `max_gap_s` defaults to 1: one missed frame does not close an event.
2. The `min_duration_s` window counts only consecutive true frames. A gap keeps open only an event that has already been raised. Before, a candidate survived a gap, and r02-vest gave an extra event on s22 (C on s19–s20, empty on s21, on s22 a welder at the column without a visible vest, not in the labels). Test `test_gap_does_not_bridge_a_candidate`.
3. Operator = a person box ≥ 50 % inside the machine box **and** the person's bottom edge is above the machine's bottom edge by ≥ 10 % of its height (`primitives.is_operator`). Test `test_r03_pedestrian_beside_reach_truck_mast_is_not_the_operator` on SAM boxes from s22 and s0.

## Addendum 27.10 evening: the "Door apron" zone and rule r11 on busy-warehouse

**Zone.** By the homography, `fire_exit_zone` on busy-warehouse was exactly 3 m deep (y 0.590–0.635, floor Y −2.54…+0.50 m). The forklift footpoint (bottom edge of the box) on s0–s9 is 4.7–7.6 m from the threshold. So the rule "a forklift does not stand in front of the gate" did not fire on any second. Decision: the bottom edge was lowered to y = 0.74 (6.47 m from the threshold by the homography), the width is the same (X 2.73–5.73 m), title "Door apron". The name `fire_exit_zone` was kept so that r05 still loads. New polygon: `[[0.4593, 0.5903], [0.6209, 0.5903], [0.8643, 0.74], [0.53, 0.74]]`, the overlay was redrawn.

**r11** (`rules/r11.json`: `in_zone(forklift, fire_exit_zone)`, 2 s, medium), second by second: `TTTT.....................`, event s1–s3 (condition from s0). Forklift footpoint: s0 y=0.674, s1 0.685, s2 0.703, s3 0.722 (inside), s4 0.7403 (0.0003 below the edge, outside), s5–s8 0.762–0.803. "Roughly s0–s6" was expected. s4–s6 fall outside because the edge is limited to 0.74. From s9 the forklift stands in the aisle, and only the load on the forks is in the zone, not the footpoint.

The rule text for the compiler had to be made more precise with a duration: "Forklifts must not stop in the door apron in front of the roll-up door." Spark compiles it with `min_duration_s = 60` (3 of 3, the prompt rule "60 s for objects left in exits"), so there is no alert on the 25-second clip. With "… for more than 2 seconds" it gives 2 s (3 of 3), live test `test_compiler_live.py 11` 2/2 PASS (row 11 in `rules-examples.md` with "for more than 2 seconds").
