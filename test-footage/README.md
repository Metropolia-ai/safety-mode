The bottom three clips were added on 30.10 from `candidates/` (selection and second-by-second check: `candidates/REPORT-2026-10-30.md`). How to get `raw/<clip>.mp4`: download `https://www.pexels.com/download/video/<id>/?w=1280` to `candidates/raw/<clip>-src.mp4`, run `python candidates/prepare.py` (trim, 10 fps video for SAM in `candidates/cache/video/`, 1 fps frames), then copy `candidates/raw/<clip>.mp4` to `raw/`, the frames to `frames/<clip>/`, the 10 fps video to `cache/video/` (the detections in git refer to it by hash). For these three the SAM video is 10 fps (billing is per frame); the first three use their native frame rate.

Zones, calibration and expected results (marked by hand on 21.10):
- `zones/<name>.json` holds the zones (polygons in frame fractions 0..1) and a 4-point floor calibration, marked by hand on frame 001. The scale assumptions are written in `floor_calibration.assumption`. `zones/draw_zones.py` (PIL only) draws `zones/<name>-overlay.jpg`; `--frame NNN` does the same on another frame, `--sheet DIR` makes contact sheets of all frames with zones. The busy-warehouse clip is handheld. After ~8 s the scene drifts by ~50 px, so the zones from frame 001 are shifted by the end of the clip (see `expected.md`).
- `expected.md` says where the alert must fire and where it must not, second by second, for rules 1–3 on all clips, 5 and 8 on busy-warehouse, 6 on warehouse-team. It also has a table of what local SAM 3.1 finds per second. The SAM 3.1 API run of 27.10 is compared with it at the end of `expected.md`; later runs are in `results-2026-10-27.md`, `results-2026-10-29.md` and `verification-2026-10-30.md`.
- `presence_from_bench.py` prints this table from the logs `../jetson-bench/logs/sam3.1-fp32_*.json` (it accounts for the 5 warm-up frames of the bench).
