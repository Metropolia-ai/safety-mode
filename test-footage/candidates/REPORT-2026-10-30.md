# Clips where the violation starts and ends inside the frame (30.10.2026)

Clip selection report, 30.10.2026. 60 clips from Pexels/Mixkit/Pixabay were reviewed, 6 downloaded, and all 6 run through SAM 3.1 (video, 10 fps) and the engine. Cost about $0.50 (12 SAM video requests, 2 196 frames, $0.44; 3 Spark calls, $0.06).

In every case the alert turns on 1–2 s after the violation starts because of the rule's `min_duration_s` (3 s for PPE, 2 s for the forklift). The detector is not the cause. For the rule texts the compiler (Muse Spark 1.3) produced the same objects, zones, condition and duration. The engine on the compiled rules produced the same intervals (`rules/compiled/`).

## Rejected
- 4280746: caps, not hard hats; SAM found no hard hats; the violation lasts all 12 s.
- 32268407: the worker is in front of the forks for 37 s of 43, one long strip; the calibration is contradictory, r03 was not run.
- 13422071: alert s12–s13, the clip ends at s13; the vest is below the threshold (the bottom of the box is hidden by a vehicle).
- The rest: moving camera, pans, close-ups, no event, or only vertical 4K without a preview.
- Pixabay returns 403; Mixkit has no static-camera clips.

## Known weaknesses
- Without masks, a neighbour's vest is credited to the person without a vest when they overlap (C, s3).
- No static-camera clips were found for the scenes "pedestrian crosses the forklift path" and "forklift stands at the gate and drives off".

## How to reproduce
Download `https://www.pexels.com/download/video/<id>/?w=1280` to `raw/<clip>-src.mp4`, then run `python prepare.py` (trim, 10 fps video for SAM, 1 fps frames), `run_candidates.py --export` (detection), `run_candidates.py` (engine, `timelines.json`), `measure.py`, `sheets.py`. `raw/`, `frames/`, `cache/` are not in git.
